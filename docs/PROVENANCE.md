# Provenance of validation values (M1 PVT and M2 gas VLP / IPR, gate G1)

Every number a unit test compares against is listed here with how it was obtained.
The tags are also used in the test comments. The M1 (PVT) section is first; the M2
section (gas VLP, gas IPR, exit choke) follows it.

| Tag | Meaning |
|---|---|
| FETCHED | Read from the named web page or PDF during the M1 or M2 session (both 21 Sep 2026). Secondary source unless the original paper is named. |
| MEMORY | Recalled from a publication. Not verified against that publication this session. |
| COMPUTED | The expected value is the equation evaluated in the test itself, or an internal-consistency or trend check. Confirms the code matches an equation form, not that the form or coefficients are right. |
| VERIFIED | Independently hand-derived or checked against a named primary or peer-reviewed source by the project owner (not by the M1/M2 sessions). The date and source are given in the row. |
| NEEDS HAND CHECK before M4 | Value or coefficient set that should be verified against the original paper or book before the black-oil blowout rate gate (G3) depends on it. |

No expected value was traced to an original paper during M1. Everything FETCHED came through a secondary source: the pengtools wiki, the zFactor R package, the GasCompressibility-py docs, a PetroSkills blog, or university lecture PDFs reproducing Ahmed's Reservoir Engineering Handbook.

## The sixth oil in the Standing Pb table

Ahmed's example has six oils. Five are in the test. The sixth (Ahmed's oil #4, which I remember as T 237 °F, Rs 968 scf/stb, gas SG 0.898, 40.5 API, measured Pb 2884 and Bo 1.619, Standing predictions 2896 and 1.618) is not.

From the remembered inputs the implementation gives Pb 3040 psia (5.0% from the remembered answer) and Bo 1.635 (1.05% from the FETCHED answer 1.618). The other five rows agree to 0.03%, so the equation is coded correctly and the disagreement is in the inputs I remembered for this row. I tried solving for a gas SG that would reproduce Pb 2896: it needs 0.952, which then makes Bo worse (1.653). No single input change fixes both, so I could not tell which remembered value is wrong. The row was dropped rather than adjusted to pass. The hand check before M4 should restore it with the book's inputs.

## What a hand check needs

- Ahmed, Reservoir Engineering Handbook, ch. 2: the six-oil table and the Standing Pb and Bo solution tables. Confirm all inputs, restore oil #4, and confirm the example numbers cited in the gas tests.
- Standing (1947) or Standing (1977/1981): the chart-annotated example (350 scf/stb, 0.75, 30 API, 200 °F) and the equation coefficients.
- Vasquez and Beggs (1980), SPE 6719: Table 1 coefficients for Rs and Bo, the co equation, and the undersaturated viscosity equation.
- A Lee-Gonzalez-Eakin worked example with a printed viscosity, from McCain (1990) ch. 6 or Ahmed ch. 2, to close the LGE gap at 2%.
- McCain (1990) ch. 16: density equation, Rsw polynomial coefficients, and viscosity ranges.
